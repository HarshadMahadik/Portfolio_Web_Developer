The site includes a parallax hero with a decorative rotating seal, a filterable menu grid (Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner / Desserts / Drinks) with vegetarian/non-veg indicators, a masonry-style photo gallery with a full lightbox viewer, a Swiper testimonials carousel, and a fully functional table reservation form with client-side validation.
